# Vinyl Collection

A personal catalogue of the vinyl records I own. A record is added by photographing its sleeve or centre label; the application identifies which pressing it is and records it against my collection.

## Language

### The records themselves

**Copy**:
A single physical record I own. Two identical pressings sitting on my shelf are two Copies.
_Avoid_: Item, Record, Album, Disc

**Release**:
A specific pressing as catalogued externally — a particular label, catalogue number, country, year and format. "Columbia CS 8163, US, 1959, stereo" is a Release. Many Copies may share one Release.
_Avoid_: Pressing, Edition, Version, Variant

**Master**:
The abstract album that spans every Release of it. *Kind of Blue* is one Master regardless of how many times it has been pressed.
_Avoid_: Album, Release Group, Title, Work

**Track**:
One piece of music at a given position on a Release, such as A2 or B1. A Track belongs to the Release, not to any Copy — every Copy of a Release has the same Tracks.
_Avoid_: Song, Cut, Number

**Collection**:
The set of every Copy I own. There is exactly one.
_Avoid_: Library, Inventory, Catalogue (see below), Shelf

### Identifying a record

**Scan**:
A single attempt to identify a Copy from a photograph of its sleeve or centre label. A Scan lasts only until it is confirmed or abandoned; it is never kept.
_Avoid_: Capture, Upload, Photo, Session

**Match Candidate**:
One of the Releases put forward as the possible answer to a Scan, shown to me alongside its sleeve image and pressing details.
_Avoid_: Result, Suggestion, Hit, Guess

**Confirmation**:
Choosing one Match Candidate, which ends the Scan and creates a Copy. Identification is never accepted without it.
_Avoid_: Accept, Approve, Select

**Catalogue Number**:
The identifier the record label prints on the sleeve and centre label. Together with the label it is the strongest evidence of which Release a record is, and the primary search key when no barcode is present.
_Avoid_: Cat No, Reference, SKU

### Enrichment

**Catalogue**:
The locally held copy of externally sourced facts — Releases and their Tracks. It mirrors an external source and may be re-fetched; nothing I have written by hand lives here except BPM.
_Avoid_: Cache, Metadata, Reference Data

**BPM**:
The tempo of a Track. It is a property of the recording, so it is held once on the Track and shared by every Copy of that Release. It is optional, and may be absent, entered by hand, or supplied by a source that is recorded alongside it.
_Avoid_: Tempo, Beats, Speed
