Record detection heuristic

The question this answers: a Stack captures hands-free — you hold up a record, the app sees it, captures it by itself, and waits for the next one. ADR 0005 decided the seeing is done by a heuristic in the browser rather than by a model, because a vision call is roughly a thousand times too slow to track a live preview. But "large, contrasty, and still for a few frames" is a description, not a tuned algorithm.

So: does it actually fire when you hold up a record, stay quiet when you don't, and refuse to fire twice on the same record? And what thresholds make that true in your real room, with your real lighting and your real records?

If the answer is no, hands-free capture is not viable and the Stack falls back to you tapping the shutter yourself. That is the decision riding on this page.

To run it — the camera needs a secure context, so serve it rather than double-clicking:

cd src/Modules/SevenBySeven.Modules.Scanning
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000/PROTOTYPE-record-detection.html
